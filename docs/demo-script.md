0:25–0:45: Send a real iMessage to the configured Spectrum identity. If not live-verified, clearly identify adapter simulation and do not claim Photon eligibility.

0:45–1:10: Show structured Gemini evidence, uncertainty and editable findings; confirm location on the map.

1:10–1:30: Open the persisted report, refresh, and add a second observation after explicitly choosing the same physical drain.

1:30–1:45: Show measured dashboard counts/time trends. Claim Tiger Data only after cloud verification.

1:45–2:00: Explain coordination and official reporting pathways. DrainDispatch is a student prototype, not a municipal or emergency service.
