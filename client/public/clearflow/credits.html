# Imagegen prompts

Built-in Imagegen tool; generated assets saved into `dist/assets/`.

## aquarium-tv.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: website hero artwork. Primary request: polished Frutiger Aero mid-2000s optimistic eco technology 3D still life. A large pearlescent white and silver rounded retro CRT television WITHOUT antennas, its screen a luminous blue aquarium with several orange goldfish and tiny neon blue tropical fish, submerged aquatic plants and sparkling clear water. A separate round glass fish bowl in front at the lower right containing two realistic orange goldfish, clear turquoise water and smooth stones. A small vivid green cluster of grass blades along the bottom. One delicate transparent floating bubble. Isolated entire scene centered, front view slightly from above, all objects fully visible, no cropping. Glossy glass, translucent blue acrylic accents, soft bright studio lighting, clean tasteful mid-2000s product advertising render, realistic refractions and material detail, charming but not cartoon. Plenty of negative space around silhouette. Actual transparent background, no backdrop, no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark. Wide 3:2 composition.

## goldfish-banner.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide website editorial banner background. Primary request: expressive close-up of a graceful orange goldfish swimming through clear turquoise blue water, Frutiger Aero aquatic optimism with a tactile printed editorial texture. Wide 2:1 composition, fish orange translucent tail sweeping across the left side and its body and eye toward the right, another smaller pale yellow fish distant upper right, a few tiny natural bubbles. Cyan teal blue background, luminous vivid orange and golden fins. Fine analog film grain and subtle halftone texture, slightly retro digitally printed photographic collage mood, not cartoon. A calm darker teal area near the center for overlaying a headline in HTML. Full bleed, no borders, no typography, no words, no UI, no logos, no watermark.

The two generated illustrations were made with OpenAI Imagegen during the project. Download the recorded image prompts. The supplied pixel logo is unchanged; its original creator and licence weren’t identified in the provided file.
